5 Year Exit Plan

The goal of this co-ownership is to serve as a springboard for both families, not a permanent sharing arrangement. Move the slider below to see how home appreciation and paying down the mortgage principal build the value needed to fund a clean buyout at Year 5.

At the 5-year mark, either family can choose to exit. The staying family refinances the home to cash out the leaving family's equity (their share of the down payment, principal paid off, and accumulated home appreciation). Rent Equalization

We choose our overall ownership split at the start (such as 50/50 or 60/40), which determines our share of the down payment and joint expenses. But since the upstairs is larger and more valuable than the downstairs suite, how do we make the living arrangement completely fair?

To keep things fair, a local appraiser determines the market rental value of both the upstairs and downstairs spaces. This appraisal accounts not only for things like square footage and bedrooms, but also the convenience of having the front door, the ceiling heights, etc.

Since one family will occupy a more valuable space than their ownership share allows, they pay a monthly 'Rent Equalization' adjustment to the other family. This monthly offset guarantees that each family's net housing benefit (their living space value plus or minus cash adjustments) matches their exact ownership percentage.
