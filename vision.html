Vision

01

Team Up for the Upgrade

Due to first-time homebuyer demand, the oldest and cheapest homes start around $375,000, yet pushing past that baseline earns outsized returns. Doubling the budget consistently unlocks exponential value: you get more than double the square footage, a drastically newer build, less than half the maintenance costs, and a far superior location. Instead of competing for overpriced properties in the lower budget tiers, we pool our resources to get more for every dollar.

02

The Stable Foundation

Need help with your down payment? Need a co-borrower? By teaming up, our financial stability and purchasing power are instantly multiplied. Homes are going up by thousands of dollars each month, pushing traditional ownership further out of reach. Instead of waiting 5 years to afford a home—when prices could be tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars higher—we use our combined strength to secure a premium asset and start building wealth right now.

03

Live Privately, Split Fairly

Our specific target is a premium single-family home featuring a main living space alongside a completely separate, functional daylight basement apartment (an ADU). Both families get their own exclusive, private living space to enjoy. We become neighbors - not roommates. Because the upstairs and downstairs aren't worth the same, we use a straightforward "Rent Equalization" formula. This means the monthly mortgage contributions are adjusted based on the market value of the space you occupy, ensuring no one is subsidizing the other's living standard.

04

The 5-Year Win

This isn't a permanent living arrangement; it's a strategic stepping stone. At the five-year mark, we trigger a defined exit strategy. Our objective is to leverage the accumulated equity to fund the purchase of a second home, allowing us to duplicate the model and gain full independence. Alternatively, the agreement includes a clear buyout mechanism and early exit paths that are 100% fair to both owners should either of us need them at any point.

Proposal Letter

We started exploring this approach because the traditional path to buying a home in today's market often forces people to compromise on the size, location, and comfort of where they live.

Jared has been investing in Utah real estate since 2017 (co-owning properties in Heber, Herriman, and Ogden, and serving on a 64-unit HOA board). These experiences taught us just how powerful real estate is for building long-term security. Our plan is built on a few core ideas: First, getting into a home now is key to start building equity early. Second, by pooling our budgets, we can get far more home value (in quality, square footage, age, and location) than we could individually. And third, if we buy a home with a separate basement suite, we can build equity together for five years and then use that growth to buy a second home. It's a springboard to getting both families into very desirable homes!

This website is an introduction to how these principles work. It also outlines the co-ownership numbers, the daily logistics, the legal protections, and our 5-year exit plan. We invite you to look through it, play with the numbers, and see if we can build something great together.

Combining Power

We don't have to wait decades or compromise on safety and comfort to own a home we love. By pooling our resources, we can step into a beautiful, move-in-ready home in a great neighborhood right now, bypassing the competitive and overpriced starter-home market.

This works because of a simple market reality: home quality does not increase at the same rate as the price. In Utah's current market, entry-level single-family homes (around $375,000) are in the highest demand, meaning buyers pay a premium for properties that are often older, smaller, or need significant repairs.

If we combine our budgets to target a $750,000 home, the value we get is far more than double. We gain access to much larger layouts, higher-quality construction, and quieter neighborhoods. While a massive pool of buyers fights over the cheapest entry-level properties, larger homes with built-in private suites (Downstairs Apartments) remain much more reasonably priced. Together, we can buy a better home, on better terms, without the bidding wars.

Getting Two Homes

$375,000

Typical Starter Home

The high-demand starter tier where buyers face intense bidding wars for compromised properties.

  • Older Construction: Built 1970s–1990s; needs repairs or modern updates.
  • Limited Space: ~1,200–1,500 sq. ft. layouts with cramped bedrooms.
  • Zoning & Noise: Denser subdivisions, busier streets, zero structural layout separation.
  • No Downstairs Potential: Lacks separate entrance, walk-out, or zoning for an independent downstairs suite.
Value Multiplier
$750,000 (Combined)

Premium Co-Owned Home

A luxury tier with far lower buyer competition and superior long-term appreciation.

  • Modern Quality: Turnkey, move-in ready (built 2020s), premium finishes.
  • Expansive Layouts: ~3,000 – 3,500 sq. ft. with high ceilings and spacious lots.
  • Daylight Downstairs: Independent basement daylight apartment with private entrance, full kitchen, & laundry.
  • Quiet Subdivisions: Larger dedicated lot, quieter street, and better appreciation potential.
