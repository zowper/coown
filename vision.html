Vision

A collaborative strategy to pool resources, get more home per dollar, live privately, and build long-term wealth together.

Combining Power

We don't have to wait decades or compromise on safety and comfort to own a home we love. By pooling our resources, we can step into a beautiful, move-in-ready home in a great neighborhood right now, bypassing the competitive and overpriced starter-home market.

This works because of a simple market reality: home quality does not increase at the same rate as the price. In Utah's current market, entry-level single-family homes (around $375,000) are in the highest demand, meaning buyers pay a premium for properties that are often older, smaller, or need significant repairs.

If we combine our budgets to target a $750,000 home, the value we get is far more than double. We gain access to much larger layouts, higher-quality construction, and quieter neighborhoods. While a massive pool of buyers fights over the cheapest entry-level properties, larger homes with built-in private suites (Downstairs Apartments) remain much more reasonably priced. Together, we can buy a better home, on better terms, without the bidding wars.

Typical Starter Home

The high-demand starter tier where buyers face intense bidding wars for compromised properties.

  • Older Construction: Built 1970s–1990s; needs repairs or modern updates.
  • Limited Space: ~1,200–1,500 sq. ft. layouts with cramped bedrooms.
  • Zoning & Noise: Denser subdivisions, busier streets, zero structural layout separation.
  • No Downstairs Potential: Lacks separate entrance, walk-out, or zoning for an independent downstairs suite.
Value Multiplier
$750,000 (Combined)

Premium Co-Owned Home

A luxury tier with far lower buyer competition and superior long-term appreciation.

  • Modern Quality: Turnkey, move-in ready (built 2020s), premium finishes.
  • Expansive Layouts: ~3,000 – 3,500 sq. ft. with high ceilings and spacious lots.
  • Daylight Downstairs: Independent basement daylight apartment with private entrance, full kitchen, & laundry.
  • Quiet Subdivisions: Larger dedicated lot, quieter street, and better appreciation potential.
