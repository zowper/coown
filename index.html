Principles

The core real estate and financial principles behind building long-term security together.

Building real equity in single-family homes on dedicated lots is the single most powerful tool for long-term financial stability. Renting represents a 100% loss of capital, while condos and townhomes share land ownership and carry high monthly Homeowner Association (HOA) fees that reduce your borrowing power and offer zero equity return.

By pooling our resources to buy a premium single-family home with a private suite, we bypass the competitive starter-home market, eliminate HOA fees, and secure double the value. This down payment multiplier and land ownership structure allows us to build significant wealth together.

Setup

The living arrangements, organizational roles, and legal protections that govern our co-ownership.

Dual Living & Legal Safeguards

This isn't about living together as roommates. It is a structured property co-ownership designed to help both households build equity while enjoying complete space and privacy. We focus on homes that naturally offer two separate, fully private living areas—typically a main level home (Upstairs) and a fully finished daylight basement (Downstairs), each with its own independent entrance, full kitchen, laundry room, and living areas.

To protect everyone's investment, our co-ownership is governed by a Good Neighbor Agreement outlining daily logistics and a formal legal Operating Agreement. This contract defines ownership percentages, defaults, and a Payment Safety Net (Dynamic Ledger) to keep our joint venture safe, clear, and predictable.

Numbers

The mathematical models governing both our daily housing equalization and our 5-year exit equity buyout.

5-Year Exit & Rent Equalization

This co-ownership is a springboard, not a permanent arrangement. At the 5-year mark, a clean exit protocol allows either family to move. The staying family refinances the property to buy out the departing family's equity (down payment, paid-off principal, and home appreciation) in cash, giving both families a solid launchpad into their next homes.

On a monthly basis, we keep things perfectly fair through Rent Equalization. A professional local appraiser sets the rental value for both the upstairs and downstairs suites. The family occupying the more valuable space pays a monthly adjustment to the other family. This offset guarantees that each family's out-of-pocket housing costs align exactly with their equity ownership share.

FAQ

Clear, direct answers on how we handle changing life circumstances and the 5-year term exit.

Q: What happens if only one family wants to move at the 5-year mark?
A: The staying family will buy out the departing family's equity share based on a professional third-party appraisal. The leaving family receives their full equity share (down payment, appreciation, and paid-off principal) in cash, giving them a clean, funded launch into their next home.

Q: What happens if both families want to stay at the 5-year mark?
A: To resolve this fairly, we use a Valuation Buyout Protocol. One family suggests a total home value. The other family then has the absolute right to either buy out the first family's share or sell their own share to them at that price. This structure guarantees a completely fair, market-accurate number.
