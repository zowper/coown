How it Works
01

Team Up for the Upgrade

Doubling the budget past the $375k starter baseline consistently unlocks far more than double the value in: square footage, home age, maintenance costs, and the superior location.

Due to first-time homebuyer demand, the oldest and cheapest homes start around $375,000, yet pushing past that baseline earns outsized returns.

Doubling the budget consistently unlocks far greater value: you get more than double the square footage, a drastically newer build, less than half the maintenance costs, and a far superior location. Instead of competing for overpriced properties in the lower budget tiers, we pool our resources to get more for every dollar.

02

The Stable Foundation

Instead of waiting 5 years while prices rise, we combine our purchasing power to multiply our stability, buy a premium home, and start building wealth today.

Need help with your down payment? Need a co-borrower? By teaming up, our financial stability and purchasing power are instantly multiplied. Homes are going up by thousands of dollars each month, pushing traditional ownership further out of reach.

Instead of waiting 5 years to afford a home—when prices could be tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars higher—we use our combined strength to buy a premium home and start building wealth right now.

03

Live Privately, Split Fairly

We target a home with a private daylight basement suite (ADU). We are neighbors, not roommates, and use a fair "Rent Equalization" offset to keep monthly costs perfectly balanced.

Our specific target is a premium single-family home featuring a main living space alongside a completely separate, functional daylight basement apartment (an ADU). Both families get their own exclusive, private living space to enjoy. We become neighbors - not roommates.

Because the upstairs and downstairs units have different rental values, we use a straightforward "Rent Equalization" formula. By adjusting monthly contributions based on the market value of the space each family occupies, we keep our out-of-pocket housing costs perfectly fair, transparent, and balanced.

04

The 5-Year Win

A defined 5-year exit strategy allows either family to buy out the other or sell. We use joint equity growth as a springboard to fund the purchase of our next separate homes.

This isn't a permanent living arrangement; it's a smart stepping stone. At the five-year mark, we trigger a defined exit strategy.

Our goal is to use the accumulated equity to fund the purchase of a second home, allowing us to duplicate the model and gain full independence. Otherwise, the agreement includes a clear buyout mechanism and early exit paths that are 100% fair to both owners should either of us need them at any point.

Now, we want to bring those same wealth-building principles to our own housing, sharing the benefits with those we care about.

When we ran the numbers, we realized the smartest, most stable path forward is a structured, 5-year co-ownership. By teaming up, two households can lift each other up and get both families into their own homes.

Our plan is built on three core principles:

  • Time in the market: Getting into a home now is the key to building equity early.
  • Pooling power: By combining our budgets, we unlock far more home value—better quality, larger square footage, newer builds, and superior locations—than we could individually.
  • The Springboard effect: By purchasing a home with a completely separate, private daylight basement suite, we can build equity together for five years. We then use that growth to fund a second home, successfully springboarding both families into desirable properties.

Principles

By pooling our resources to buy a premium single-family home on a dedicated lot, we bypass competitive starter-home bidding wars and avoid monthly HOA fees. This allows both families to build real equity and wealth much faster than renting or buying alone.

Rent vs. Buy: Renting or buying starter townhomes limits wealth building, while co-owning a premium home generates over $100,000 in equity per family over five years.

Owning Land & HOAs: Owning the underlying land builds lasting value, whereas condos and townhomes share land and charge costly HOA fees that drain borrowing power.

Time in Market: Getting into a home early allows your investment to compound, avoiding the high cost of waiting as home prices rise.

Premium vs. Starter Homes: Combining budgets bypasses over-competitive starter home bidding wars to unlock newer, lower-maintenance properties.

ADU Safety Buffer: Target properties naturally feature a private suite, providing a built-in rental income buffer or safety net.

Down Payment Multiplier: Doubling our down payment capacity lets us secure a high-performing asset that accelerates joint equity growth.
